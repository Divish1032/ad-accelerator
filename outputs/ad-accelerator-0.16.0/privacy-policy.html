Privacy policy

Effective September 7, 2026 · Ad Accelerator 0.16.0
Developer: Divyansh Kumar · divyansh1032@gmail.com

Ad Accelerator processes information in your browser to reduce ads, tracking, and unwanted popup navigation. The extension has no account system, analytics service, advertising, or developer-operated data collection server. It does not sell your data.

Information used in your browser

The extension inspects website addresses, navigation events, links, visible page elements, and video-player state to apply protection. It can change page presentation and detected ad playback. It does not read passwords, payment fields, cookie values, or location coordinates. It does not request Chrome’s browsing-history permission.

Local extension storage contains settings, site exceptions, temporary pause times, strict-mode destinations, optional location-blocked origins, custom CSS selectors for elements you choose to hide, filter lists, and filter-update status. These settings are not synchronized or sent to the developer.

Automatic learning and temporary activity

Adaptive protection stores up to 100 candidate or active rules and up to 100 temporary allow pairs locally. A rule contains source, destination and evidence hostnames, counts, timestamps and expiry. It does not persist full browsing URLs, query strings or page text. Rules expire 24 hours after first evidence; allow pairs expire after 24 hours. Expired rules are not enforced and are pruned periodically.

Pending popup chains may contain a source URL and tab identifiers in bounded worker memory for up to 10 seconds. Short-lived popup activity and navigation attempts are kept in memory or session storage, not uploaded. Private windows are excluded from adaptive learning. Video transition evidence stays in page memory and resets on navigation or page shutdown. While you use the element picker, a source URL and a tab identifier can remain in session storage for up to five minutes to validate your selection.

Network connections

The extension downloads three domain lists from HaGeZi’s public GitHub repository when you update filters or when enabled daily updates run. Those requests contain no browsing history, learned rules, cookie values, or page contents. The list host receives ordinary connection metadata, including your IP address. Downloads omit credentials and referrers. EasyList cosmetic filters are bundled with the extension. Downloaded lists are data, not executable code.

The extension does not proxy your browsing, hide your IP address, or prevent every form of tracking. Websites and their providers still receive ordinary traffic that is not blocked.

Optional permissions

  • Chrome privacy settings: used only to request a third-party cookie block and read its effective status. Turning it off removes our override. We do not retrieve cookie contents.
  • Content settings: used to block location requests from a selected website and its same-origin frames. We do not obtain your location.
  • Browsing data: used only after you review and confirm selected site-data deletion. Chrome performs the deletion. Cookies can be cleared for related subdomains; no cookie values are read by the extension.

Cleanup confirmations are one-use, tied to the source tab and origin, expire after five minutes, and are kept in bounded session storage. No data is deleted automatically. Deletion cannot be undone.

Your choices and retention

You can pause or disable a site, turn learning off, allow or clear learned rules, undo hidden elements, disable list updates, and remove optional privacy overrides. A full site pause also releases our Chrome-wide cookie override while any full site pause or exception remains. Saved choices are retained for restoration. Underlying Chrome settings still apply.

Settings and saved filter data remain locally until changed or the extension is uninstalled. Uninstalling removes the extension’s local storage and its Chrome-setting overrides. It cannot undo site-data deletion or erase copies you exported yourself.

This website and support email

This public website has no advertising, analytics scripts, sign-in form, or tracking cookies added by us. Its hosting providers process ordinary requests and may keep operational logs such as IP addresses and requested URLs under their own policies. Contacting support sends your message and email address to the developer’s Gmail inbox. They are used to respond and troubleshoot, not for marketing or sale. You may request deletion of support correspondence using the same email address.

Use limitations and updates

Any use of information received from Google APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is used only for the user-facing features described here. We do not use browsing information for unrelated advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or sale.

This policy will be updated if the extension’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. For questions, contact divyansh1032@gmail.com.
