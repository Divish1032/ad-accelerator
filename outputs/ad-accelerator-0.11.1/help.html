Help & support

Ad Accelerator is a free Chrome extension by Divyansh Kumar. It blocks listed ad and tracker destinations, hides common ad boxes, and tries to shorten detected video ads.

The Chrome Web Store release is being prepared. There is no public store installation link yet. This extension requires Chrome 145 or later.

Your everyday controls

  1. Open a website and select Ad Accelerator from Chrome’s extensions menu. Pin it for quicker access.
  2. Use Protection for this site to keep protection on or off for the current hostname and its subdomains. The choice is remembered.
  3. Use Pause here for 10 minutes for a temporary break. Previously enabled site protections return when the timer ends. Reload to retry requests that were already blocked.
  4. Under Video ads, choose a speed and whether to try skipping first. Normal playback speed is restored when the detected ad ends. The 20× option is a request; Chrome generally caps playback at 16× and players may allow less.

If a page stops working

Pause protection for that site and reload. For a smaller change, choose Page not working? and pause just the affected feature. Undo manually hidden boxes under My hidden elements.

Strict navigation is optional. It restricts external destinations on a chosen site and can interrupt sign-ins or embedded players. Allow the exact destination or turn strict mode off, then reload.

What pausing means

A full site pause suspends network filtering, page hiding, video assistance, popup cleanup, adaptive protection, strict navigation, tracking-link cleanup, and our location restrictions for matching sites. Saved preferences stay in place.

If you enabled our third-party cookie control, any full site pause or lasting exception also suspends that override across Chrome. It returns only after all full site pauses and exceptions end, if still enabled. Chrome’s own restrictions continue to apply. Filter-list updates have their own setting; pausing a site does not delete data or reverse prior deletions.

Optional privacy tools

Privacy controls let you clean recognized tracking parameters, ask Chrome to block third-party cookies, block location on a selected origin, or review site data before deleting it. Extra permissions are requested when needed. Clearing cookies can sign you out of related subdomains; deleting site data cannot be undone.

Automatic learning

Adaptive protection is on by default. It learns narrow, local rules after repeated popup chains reach an already listed destination. Rules expire after 24 hours. It does not use an external AI service. You can allow a mistaken rule or clear learning and turn it off.
