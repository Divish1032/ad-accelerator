## Short description

## Detailed description

Spend less time on ad breaks and keep control of how protection works on each website.

Ad Accelerator is a free Chrome extension that filters listed advertising and tracking requests, hides common ad boxes, limits unwanted popup redirects, and shortens video ads it can confidently detect. It does not require an account.

Main features:

• Skip or speed up detected video ads. The extension tries a genuine ad-specific Skip control first. If the ad remains, it uses your selected 2×, 4×, 8×, 10×, or 20× speed and restores normal playback afterward. Chrome generally caps the last option at 16×, and some players allow less.

• Named on-demand video integrations for Prime Video, JioHotstar, YouTube, Plex, Amazon MX Player, ZEE5, SonyLIV and WeTV. A conservative generic detector can also recognize clear ad transitions on some other HTML5 players. Broader compatibility is experimental, not guaranteed.

• Site-by-site controls. Keep protection on or off for a hostname and its subdomains, or pause every feature there for 10 minutes. Your saved preferences remain available.

• Ad, tracker, popup and threat filtering from packaged and user-updated domain lists. You can also hide a page element yourself and undo it later.

• Optional Strict navigation for websites with unwanted external redirects. Exact destination allowances and recovery controls help when a page needs an exception.

• Local adaptive popup protection. Narrow rules are learned only after repeated popup chains reach already listed destinations. Learned rules expire after 24 hours and can be allowed or cleared.

• Optional privacy tools for recognized tracking parameters, Chrome’s third-party-cookie setting, location requests on a selected origin, and reviewed site-data deletion. Extra permissions are requested only when needed.

Privacy and limits:

Settings and limited learning data stay in your browser. There is no usage analytics service, developer-operated collection server, external AI service, or sale of browsing data. Filter lists are downloaded as data from public GitHub sources; those hosts receive ordinary connection metadata.

Live and FAST channels, audio services, server-inserted ads, inaccessible cross-origin players, protected timelines and ads without reliable player signals may remain unchanged. Website changes can also affect compatibility. If a page stops working, pause protection for that site and reload.

Ad Accelerator does not unlock subscriptions, hide your IP address, or guarantee that every ad, tracker, redirect, or unsafe website is blocked. It is independent software and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the named streaming services.

Requires Chrome 145 or later.
