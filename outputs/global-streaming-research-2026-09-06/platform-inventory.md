# Global streaming platform coverage inventory

Research date: 6 September 2026. Prepared for Ad Accelerator's Chrome expansion.

This is a broad engineering discovery map, not a claim to enumerate every streaming site worldwide. It includes important international services, regional broadcasters, music/radio/podcast players and non-graphic adult-service candidates. No live playback, account access, extension compatibility or successful ad skipping was tested during this research. Availability can depend on country, subscription, device, age eligibility, title and date.

The market column identifies a home market or useful research grouping, **not an exhaustive availability list**. A domain is a starting point, not a verified manifest match pattern or CDN block rule. Regional sites, redirects, embeds and carrier login requirements still need checking. Do not block the listed domains wholesale: they are content services, not a blocklist.

## How to read the catalog

- **F**: an official source documents a free ad-supported offering.
- **M**: sources document a mixed free/paid or otherwise mixed ad-supported offering. Check the note; this does not mean all content is free.
- **P**: a paid ad-supported offering is documented. A subscription is still required.
- **B**: broadcast commercial breaks are documented, but separately controllable ad media is not established.
- **A**: adult advertising inventory is documented by an ad-network source; service access/player details remain to be qualified.
- **U**: a discovery candidate, **not a confirmed current ad-supported Chrome target**. Some are paid, may be ad-free, have limited browser access, have ambiguous current branding, or may no longer operate. Partial sources establish only the fact described in the note. Rows without sources are an explicit research backlog, not verified findings.

Official help, plan, privacy, corporate and developer app descriptions were preferred. A source about an app does not establish desktop parity. Ad-network evidence establishes advertising inventory, not player architecture. A free trial, free app download, carrier bundle or zero-rated data plan is not a permanent free viewing tier. Source pages can themselves be stale; conflicts are noted rather than silently resolved.

This inventory intentionally separates positive model evidence from the broader coverage backlog. It is not a popularity ranking. No claim is made that every candidate is currently active or that all major services have been captured. Public-service and subscription-only controls appear in the companion research report.

**Inventory:** 499 service/regional surfaces; 113 with documented ad models or inventory and 386 qualification candidates. Counts are not unique companies or independently implemented players.
