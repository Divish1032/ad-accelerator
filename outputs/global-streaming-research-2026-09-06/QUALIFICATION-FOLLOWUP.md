## What this changes for the extension backlog

My recommendation is to move Vidio, Tonton, mewatch, CBC Gem, Joyn Austria and Atresplayer into the shortlist for **native on-demand ad observation**, where a tester has the appropriate country and account. This is an engineering judgment based on documented offerings, not a popularity ranking or a guarantee of easy skipping.

Keep ZEE5 in the paid-with-ads pool unless a specific free title is separately verified. Keep Samsung TV Plus and LG Channels in the live/FAST track. Fawesome's advertising model is confirmed, but its cited device page emphasizes TV/mobile distribution; desktop qualification should precede an adapter.

The mewatch advertising specification explicitly distinguishes skippable and non-skippable inventory. Tonton's plan table distinguishes ads in free shows from local live-channel ads. Both are useful reminders that one hostname can contain several ad behaviors. A platform's premium ad-free promise does not establish that every live channel or free-catalog title is ad-free.

## Remaining gaps

This pass also searched for firmer evidence on The CW, Runtime and several regional services without resolving their current browser ad model. Empty search results do not establish that a service lacks ads. Do not promote these entries on the basis of a free app download, a promotional trial, an old package article, or a distributor's player alone.

The full 386-entry candidate pool remains a research backlog. None should be advertised as supported by Ad Accelerator. The next useful research pass is to verify the most accessible remaining broadcaster and regional video candidates, then qualify audio-browser behavior separately. Adult candidates remain as documented in the original report; this pass adds no new adult-site findings.
